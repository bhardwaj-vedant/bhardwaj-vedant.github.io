Outside research, much of my free time goes to problems of a different kind. I regularly solve competitive
programming problems on **Codeforces** and from the **USACO** archive. I am particularly drawn to **dynamic
programming** and **graph algorithms**, where a problem usually turns on finding the right state or the right
structure.

I love **probability puzzles** and card games such as **poker** and **blackjack**, where careful reasoning about
odds and expected value pays off. The same interest led me to build a
[poker equity engine]({{ base_path }}/portfolio/11-poker-equity-engine/).

I also enjoy reading mathematics for its own sake, particularly probability. Working through Roman Vershynin's
*High-Dimensional Probability* was especially rewarding, and it changed how I think about randomness,
concentration, and geometry in high dimensions.

When I want to switch off, I watch films and TV shows.
