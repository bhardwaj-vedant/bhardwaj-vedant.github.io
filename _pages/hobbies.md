These are some of the interests I pursue outside research. Several of them have shaped the way I think about
research problems.

Astronomy and Astrophotography
======
I have a long-standing interest in the night sky. As a volunteer with **Krittika, the Astronomy Club of IIT
Bombay**, I helped organise an astrophotography exhibition for National Space Day. I also wrote a Learners' Space
course on spectroscopy and photometry, which taught me a good deal about extracting reliable signal from noisy
measurements.

Reading Mathematics
======
I enjoy reading mathematics for its own sake, particularly probability. Working through Roman Vershynin's
*High-Dimensional Probability* was especially rewarding, and it changed how I think about randomness,
concentration, and geometry in high dimensions.

Competitive Programming
======
I enjoy algorithmic problem solving and have solved more than 300 problems on **LeetCode** and the **CSES
Problem Set**, mostly on graphs, trees, and dynamic programming.

Games of Strategy and Chance
======
I like games that reward careful reasoning under uncertainty. This interest led me to build a
[high-performance poker equity engine]({{ base_path }}/portfolio/11-poker-equity-engine/) and a
[Minimax agent for Microchess]({{ base_path }}/portfolio/13-rl-decision-making/). It also explains my interest
in game theory, which I studied formally as a course.

Reverse Engineering and Security
======
I take part in **Crack-Me** challenges to practise reverse engineering and binary analysis. Cybersecurity is a
long-standing side interest.

Robotics and Aeromodelling
======
In my first year I competed in teams of four in the Aeromodelling Club's **remote-controlled plane
competition** and in **XLR8**, the Electronics and Robotics Club's remote-controlled bot competition, where I
designed ESP32-based circuits.

Sports
======
I won a **bronze medal in the Freshiesta Tug of War General Championship**, representing Hostel 16B.
