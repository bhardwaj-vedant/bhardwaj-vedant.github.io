### Overview
The project compared classical statistical forecasting with deep-learning forecasting on financial data. It
also examined how far forecasting can go, given the Random Walk Hypothesis.

### What I did

- Implemented simple exponential smoothing, Holt's linear trend, and **Holt–Winters** models to capture trend and
  seasonality in financial data.
- Analysed more than five years of market data with classical methods (**ARIMA, GARCH**) and deep models
  (**LSTM, CNN**) to study price movements and volatility.
- Studied stationarity, autocorrelation, and trend decomposition, and what the **Random Walk Hypothesis** implies
  for predicting stock prices.
